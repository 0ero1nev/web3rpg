## Conclusion

The strict participant rule works well for Jupiter and the sampled Raydium routes, with no observed false positives. Its main limitation is provider coverage: DFlow and OKX transactions frequently have transaction-level labels but no wallet-level `events.swap`. Supporting them safely requires a venue-specific, account-aware decoder or another provider that exposes authoritative user input/output legs; simply trusting the Solscan or Helius transaction label would weaken the semantic guarantee.
