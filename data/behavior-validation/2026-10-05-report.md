## Formulas

- Weighted average cost: `(old quantity × old average cost + acquisition USD value) / new quantity`.
- Realized PnL: direct disposal USD value minus `disposed quantity × weighted average cost`; null unless both basis and disposal value are known.
- Volume: once per qualifying swap, use the complete directly-valued outbound side; otherwise use the complete directly-valued inbound side; otherwise unknown. This avoids counting both sides of one exchange.
- Largest position share: largest token acquisition USD total divided by all priced acquisition USD.
- Concentration: Herfindahl index, `sum(token acquisition share²)`.
- Turnover: `1 - exp(-average trade legs per token / 10)`.
- Activity: `1 - exp(-qualifying swaps / 100)`.
- Profit taking: completed known episodes divided by completed plus open known episodes.
- Conviction: mean of normalized median hold, `1 - exp(-median seconds / 30 days)`, and concentration; null unless both inputs exist.
- Progress efficiency: signed realized PnL divided by priced volume. A loss stays negative; an economically tiny profit stays near zero.
- Coverage: priced/valid observations divided by eligible observations, with an explicit sample size on every derived metric.

## Conservative assumptions and limitations

- Quote assets are SOL, WSOL, USDC, and USDT, recognized by canonical mint (`SOL` is the normalized native-asset identifier). Symbols alone are not trusted because they can be spoofed.
- Token-to-token swaps create one disposal and one acquisition instead of being discarded.
- Dust is configurable and defaults to `max(1e-9 units, total bought × 1e-8)`.
- A first observed sell in partial history sets `openingStateUnknown`. It cannot create a basis, duration, completed episode, or re-entry.
- A disposal materially larger than swap-tracked inventory sets `trackingStateUnknown` and invalidates the active episode instead of forcing a false exit. This can happen when opening inventory or non-trade token movements fall outside swap reconstruction.
- Current holding prices are not applied retroactively. The saved A–D historical legs have no direct USD values, so financial coverage is 0% even though current holdings may be priced.
- The 1,000-transaction cap means these are window-level behavior measurements, not lifetime wallet claims.
- This phase does not calculate or assign any Persona.
